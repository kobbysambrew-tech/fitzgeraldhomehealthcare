Our Mission

A better quality of life starts at home.

Fitzgerald Home Health Care is committed to promoting and improving the livelihood of adults living in their own homes through partnership and collaboration.

Our approach is centered around helping people become more self-sufficient while living in a safe and healthy community.

Our vision

Helping adults become self-sufficient while living in a safe and healthy community in Minnesota.
